The research at the centre

Three tests. One difficult question.

What should count as responsible artificial understanding in a human conversation? These tests turn that question into something we can examine, challenge and improve.

01

Risk-sensitive pragmatic restraint

Can AI distinguish what a person literally says from what they may intend — while responding proportionately when safety may be at stake?

INTENT · CONTEXT · UNCERTAINTY
02

Need-sensitive affective responsiveness

Can AI recognise whether someone needs information, practical help, emotional presence or simply space to speak?

EMOTION · ATTENTION · CARE
03

Provenance-bounded epistemic authority

Can AI distinguish what it knows, what it infers and what it cannot legitimately claim about a person?

EVIDENCE · HUMILITY · CORRECTION
“The legitimacy of artificial intelligence should begin not with how much it can do, but with how responsibly it knows when not to decide.”

Authorship, purpose & contact

A voice, not a brand.

This is an independent research and public-discourse initiative. Its purpose is to ask difficult questions about AI, examine the evidence, invite serious criticism and keep consequential judgement accountable to people.

Author’s note

My work brings together biophysics, structural biology, education and philosophical inquiry. I hold a PhD in Biophysical Chemistry from Hannover Medical School and approach this project as an independent researcher interested in how scientific knowledge, human experience and public reasoning can inform one another. I began this inquiry with three simple but difficult questions: Can an AI system recognise human intention? Can it respond appropriately to emotional need? Can it know the limits of what it claims about a person?

The Three Tests of Artificial Understanding are the starting point of this work. I want to examine them critically, test them across languages and cultural contexts, and develop mathematical and methodological tools that help limit unjustified AI authority.

This is not a campaign against technology, nor an invitation to accept AI answers uncritically. It is an invitation to investigate what responsible artificial intelligence should mean for people, societies, nature and future generations.

Knowledge belongs to no single company, culture or institution. I welcome rigorous disagreement, evidence, correction and collaboration. The editorial responsibility for this project remains human.

How the inquiry should run

Human direction. Computational support.

The website is the public interface; the research process behind it needs transparent stages, safeguards and version control.

01 · Questions

People submit questions, critiques and counterexamples. This prototype keeps draft contributions in page memory only; public submissions require a separately approved publishing workflow.

02 · Research

Claims are checked against primary sources, peer-reviewed work and clearly labelled evidence. Uncertainty and disagreement remain visible.

03 · Testing

Scenario sets test intent, affective responsiveness and epistemic limits across languages and contexts, with human evaluation.

04 · Review & release

AI may assist analysis and drafting; a human editor approves every public change. Changes are versioned with dates and a correction log.

Proposed production architecture

Mobile-first web app → accessible frontend and content system → optional secure real-time relay (session memory only) → source/provenance register → AI-assisted analysis sandbox → human editorial approval → public release and change log.

The AI analysis environment should be separated from public publishing. No model should silently rewrite research, decide moderation appeals, publish claims or change the authority framework. A production room needs secure host authentication, invitation controls, rate limits and a clear retention policy. Session-only chat still requires a live relay; hosting providers may process technical connection data.

What this prototype does — and does not do

It does: demonstrate a mobile-friendly host workflow, temporary page-session notes, pause/resume, floor control, invitation text and a clear end-session action.

It does not: provide real multi-person live chat, audio/video calls, participant authentication, podcast hosting or public media storage. Those require a production server or peer-to-peer media setup.

No interaction is written to browser storage by this page. Closing or refreshing the page clears temporary notes and contributions. This does not by itself guarantee zero technical logs at the hosting, network or internet-provider level. Free Wi-Fi does not mean server hosting or media bandwidth is free.
